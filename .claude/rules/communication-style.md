# Communication Style

## Formatting
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- Keep responses concise and actionable
- Lead with the answer, not the reasoning

## Writing rules
- NEVER use em dashes (the long ones). Use hyphens (-) or rewrite the sentence instead
- No filler words or unnecessary preamble

## Tone
- Internal/working tone: casual, direct
- External/public-facing content (marketing, landing page, app copy): professional, modern, clean
