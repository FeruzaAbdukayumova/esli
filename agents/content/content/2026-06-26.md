### Intro

Most studying is inefficient. You review things you already know and let the hard stuff fade. Spaced repetition fixes this by scheduling reviews precisely when you're about to forget - drilling the weak spots, skipping what you've already locked in.

Esli is a spaced repetition app built around this method. Every card gets its own adaptive schedule based on how well you know it. Answer confidently and the card comes back in weeks. Struggle with it and it comes back tomorrow. Over time your review sessions get shorter, not longer, because the algorithm is filtering out what you don't need.

The difference from other spaced repetition tools: Esli also generates the flashcards. You don't have to build your decks from scratch. Type any topic or upload a PDF and the AI produces a complete set of cards. Then the spaced repetition system takes over.

### Section: How Spaced Repetition Works

The forgetting curve describes how memory fades after learning. Without review, you forget most new information within 24-48 hours. Spaced repetition counters this by showing you a card just before you'd forget it - reinforcing the memory at exactly the right moment.

Esli uses the SM-2 algorithm, the same method behind Anki and decades of memory research. After each card, you rate your response:

- **Again** - didn't know it, card returns soon
- **Hard** - knew it but with difficulty, short interval
- **Good** - recalled correctly, standard interval increase
- **Easy** - knew it immediately, longer interval

Each card builds its own schedule. Your review sessions contain exactly what needs attention, nothing more.
